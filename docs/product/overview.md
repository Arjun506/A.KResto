# Product Overview

AK Business OS is a comprehensive software platform that merges SaaS features with dynamic developer interfaces. It consists of four distinct platforms:

## 1. Business Platform
The core operator workspace containing modular business tools:
- **CRM:** Customer directories, feedback logs, and transaction timelines.
- **Inventory & POS:** Stock counters, low-stock thresholds, and multi-terminal checkout registers.
- **Accounting & HRMS:** General ledgers, payroll processes, shift schedules, and resource allocations.

## 2. Consumer Platform
Customer-facing services that tie into business logic:
- **QR portals:** Contactless checkout and dining order registries.
- **Online Checkout:** Dynamic billing gates supporting multiple payment adapters.
- **Customer Accounts:** Order timelines, transaction logs, and reward points.

## 3. Developer Platform
An extensibility toolkit:
- **Core APIs:** Unified REST and GraphQL interfaces.
- **Developer SDKs:** Helpers to trigger business actions and listen to webhook events.
- **Sandbox Environment:** Testing environments for capability verification.

## 4. AI & Marketplace
- **Unified AI System:** Text-to-SQL report generators, analytics forecasts, and natural language dashboard commands.
- **SaaS Marketplace:** A store where developers list custom industry packs or dynamic capability add-ons.
