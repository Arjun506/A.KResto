# Phase 29E — Restaurant Onboarding Audit

This document audits the 16-step onboarding wizard for Restaurant pilot operators.

---

## 1. Onboarding Wizard Wizard Steps

1. **Step 1 — Account**: Owner credential creation.
2. **Step 2 — Business Profile**: Legal name, contact details.
3. **Step 3 — Outlet**: Main location setup.
4. **Step 4 — Business Hours**: Active shift schedules.
5. **Step 5 — Taxes**: GST/VAT settings.
6. **Step 6 — Service Charges**: Default service rates.
7. **Step 7 — Order Types**: Dine-in, takeaway, delivery.
8. **Step 8 — Tables/Floors**: Dining tables coordinates.
9. **Step 9 — Menu**: Category and menu item listings.
10. **Step 10 — Staff**: Role assignment invitation.
11. **Step 11 — POS**: Register terminal authorization.
12. **Step 12 — KDS**: Kitchen display activation.
13. **Step 13 — QR Ordering**: QR code print layout templates.
14. **Step 14 — Payments**: Sandbox testing parameters.
15. **Step 15 — Review**: Summary check.
16. **Step 16 — Go Live**: Workspace set to active state.
