# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

AK Business OS represents premium, state-of-the-art enterprise software. The brand design emphasizes clean line work, geometric structures, high-contrast layouts, and dynamic motion.

## Color Principles

Our visual interface relies on HSL color mapping supporting both light and dark systems.

- **Primary Brand Color (Indigo/Violet):** Represents innovation, integration, and platform intelligence.
- **Success Tone (Emerald/Green):** Represents completed actions, operational safety, and billing validations.
- **Warning Tone (Amber/Orange):** Indicates low-stock thresholds, pending approvals, and configuration alerts.
- **Danger Tone (Rose/Red):** Identifies critical access blocks, payment failures, and system errors.

## Typography

We utilize **Inter** (sans-serif) for system dashboards and configuration widgets, and **Outfit** for platform marketing, developer headers, and dashboard metrics.

- **Headers:** H1 (32px, bold), H2 (24px, semibold), H3 (18px, medium).
- **Body Text:** 14px/16px with clear line height (1.5) to optimize readability on desktop and mobile.
