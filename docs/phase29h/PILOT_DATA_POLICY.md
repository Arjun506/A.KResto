# Phase 29H — Pilot Data Policy

This policy governs the retention, encryption, and cleanup procedures for pilot transactional data.

---

## 1. Data Retention & Isolation

- **Consent**: Pilot owner consent required prior to storing real operational transaction data.
- **Sensitive Fields**: Passwords, OTPs, and customer metadata must be encrypted at rest.
- **Data Cleanup**: Synthetic test records are deleted post-verification, keeping real pilot logs isolated.
