# Phase 30 Wave 5 — Industry Onboarding Engine

---

## Dynamic Onboarding Step Resolution

When a tenant owner creates a business:
1. **Industry Selection**: Owner picks industry family and business type.
2. **Capability Recommendation**: System pre-activates recommended capabilities (e.g. POS + Menu + Tables for Restaurant).
3. **Pack-Specific Setup Injections**:
   - Restaurant ➔ Add dishes & configure table layout.
   - Retail ➔ Add products & configure barcode scanner.
   - Hotel ➔ Add rooms & set check-in times.
   - Healthcare ➔ Add OPD services & doctor rosters.
