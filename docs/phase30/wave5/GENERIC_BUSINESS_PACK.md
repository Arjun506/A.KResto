# Phase 30 Wave 5 — Generic Business Pack

## Custom Business Mode Specifications

For long-tail businesses that do not fall neatly into a pre-configured industry pack:
- **Default Capabilities**: CRM, Inventory, Appointments, Tasks, Commerce, Staff Roster, Invoicing, Document Storage.
- **Custom Capability Toggles**: Business owners can freely enable or disable capability modules according to operational needs.
