# Phase 30 Wave 5 — Cross-Industry Architecture

---

## Multi-Pack Business & Group Portfolio Composition

- **Multi-Pack Business**: Single business entity activating multiple industry capability packs (e.g. Hotel + Restaurant + Spa + Gift Shop). Shared CRM, inventory, and payment engines prevent data duplication.
- **Multi-Business Organization**: Group hierarchy running separate business divisions across different industries (e.g. Restaurant Division + Retail Division + Hotel Division).
