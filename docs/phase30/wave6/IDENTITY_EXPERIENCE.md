# Phase 30 Wave 6 — Identity Experience Specifications

---

## Universal Identity & Context Switcher Blueprint

- **Single Identity Model**: One user account spans Customer, Business Owner, Worker, and Partner roles.
- **Context Switcher**: Explicit persona selector in top navigation bar (`Consumer Mode`, `Business Owner Mode`, `Employee Worker Mode`, `Delivery Partner Mode`).
- **Security Isolation**: Persona switching re-evaluates RBAC claims and tenant context boundaries without exposing restricted data.
