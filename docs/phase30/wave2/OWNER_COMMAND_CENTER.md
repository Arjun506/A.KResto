# Phase 30 Wave 2 — Owner Command Center Architecture

---

## Executive Operating Surface Specifications

The Owner Command Center transforms `/dashboard` into an executive multi-location control tower for business owners.

### Information Hierarchy & Layout Sections
1. **Workspace Header & Multi-Location Selectors**:
   - Organization ➔ Business ➔ Location hierarchy filter.
   - Date range and active industry pack dropdown.
2. **Business Health Summary**:
   - Total Revenue, Today's Sales, Active Members, Average Order Value, & Health Score.
3. **Needs Attention Center**:
   - Severity-classified alerts (`CRITICAL`, `WARNING`, `INFO`) aggregating low inventory, overdue invoices, pending approvals, and booking conflicts.
4. **Today's Operations Panel**:
   - Staff shifts, supplier deliveries, reservations, and pending owner tasks.
5. **AI Owner Insights**:
   - Anomaly detection, peak hour predictions, and stock optimization suggestions with explicit user confirmation requirements.
