## Universal Partner Shell Blueprint

AK Partner OS handles external service providers, delivery riders, mobility drivers, and contractors:

### Primary Navigation
- `Home`: Online/offline availability toggle, active delivery/ride offers, and current trip stats.
- `Jobs`: Available job marketplace and accepted assignment queue.
- `Earnings`: Daily/weekly breakdown and withdrawal requests.
- `Chat`: Contextual customer and dispatch communication.
- `Profile`: Vehicle details, verification status, and support options.
