## Consumer Connectivity & Offline State Specifications

- **Connectivity Banners**: Simple user-facing banners for `Online`, `Nearby Mode`, and `Offline`.
- **Nearby Mode**: Allows discovering locally cached menus and catalogs when global internet is disconnected.
- **Offline Operations**: Local queueing of draft orders with explicit "Pending Sync" status indicators.
