## Multi-Vertical Category Registry

AK Customer OS supports multi-category discovery across 14 initial service verticals:

1. **Food & Dining**: Restaurants, cafes, dark kitchens, QR dining.
2. **Shopping**: Grocery stores, retail shops, electronics.
3. **Rides & Mobility**: Taxis, ride hailing, bike shares.
4. **Hotels & Stays**: Boutique hotels, resorts, room bookings.
5. **Salon & Beauty**: Hair salons, spas, wellness appointments.
6. **Healthcare**: Clinics, OPD consultation bookings.
7. **Home Services**: Electricians, plumbers, repair technicians.
8. **Housekeeping**: Room cleaning, linen service, maid bookings.
9. **Travel**: Trip packages, local tours, itineraries.
10. **Events**: Concerts, conferences, event ticket bookings.
11. **Fitness**: Gym passes, trainer sessions.
12. **Laundry**: Pickup & drop cleaning services.
13. **Automotive**: Car servicing, washes.
14. **Pet Care**: Grooming, veterinary appointments.
