# Phase 29G — Pilot Owner Quickstart Guide

Welcome to the AK Business OS controlled pilot program. This guide will walk you through your initial setup.

---

## 1. Onboarding Quick Steps

1. **Accept Invitation**: Click your invitation link.
2. **Create Admin Account**: Enter your email and password to register your administrator profile.
3. **Register Workspace**: Input your restaurant's display name, business hours, and tax rates.
4. **Build Your Menu**: Add categories (e.g. Beverages, Mains) and insert your menu items.
5. **Set Tables**: Add dining table IDs and capacities.
6. **Open POS Register**: Open your cashier POS layout.
7. **Kitchen Monitor**: Load your KDS monitor in the kitchen.
8. **QR Codes**: Scan the generated table QR code to test order placement.
