# Company Overview

## Vision & Philosophy

AK Corporation is dedicated to building the operational fabric for modern businesses. We believe that traditional SaaS platforms are too fragmented, forcing organizations to stitch together disparate tools for billing, CRM, POS, inventory, and HR. 

Our vision is a unified **Business Operating System** (AK Business OS) that serves as the single source of truth for corporate logic, customer journeys, operations, and intelligence.

## Culture & Mission

- **Unified over Siloed:** Build tools that naturally share contexts, database attributes, and user workflows.
- **AI Native:** Embed reasoning capabilities, natural language search interfaces, and automation models directly into the corporate runtime.
- **Extensible Frameworks:** Empower developer ecosystems to build industry-specific customizations (Industry Packs) and list them on our Marketplace.
- **Tenant Autonomy & Safety:** Keep corporate security, privacy, and data isolation at the core of all architectural decisions.
