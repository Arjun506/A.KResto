# Roadmap: Future Roadmap

This document outlines long-term visual goals beyond v1.0.0.

## 1. Advanced AI Agents
- **Objective:** Enable AI models to make purchase decisions, drafts, and automatically schedule employee shifts based on booking volumes.
- **Timeline:** H2 2027

## 2. Multi-Region Database Partitioning
- **Objective:** Support high-volume corporate organizations by partition scaling databases to local regions (e.g. EU, APAC, US) to maintain compliance and fast query speeds.
- **Timeline:** Q1 2028

## 3. Hardware Integration Kits
- **Objective:** Partner with payment terminal hardware vendors to ship pre-flashed checkout devices that link directly with our POS API modules.
- **Timeline:** Q3 2028
