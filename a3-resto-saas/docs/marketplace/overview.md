# SaaS Marketplace Overview

This document details the Marketplace structure for custom developer extensions on **AK Business OS**.

## 1. Product Concept

The Marketplace allows verified developer entities to distribute specialized features, integrations, and customization templates:
- **Capability Add-ons:** Modular features (e.g. dynamic tax calculators, custom shipment integrations).
- **Industry Packs:** Templates that preload business entities, roles, checklists, and visual schemas (e.g. Healthcare clinic templates, Salon checkouts).

## 2. Revenue Sharing Framework

- **Billing Engine Integration:** Subscriptions process through the core system billing pipelines.
- **Rev-Share Split:** Standard 80/20 split (80% to developers, 20% commission fee).
- **Audit Logs:** Logs catalog transactions and payment dispersals to developers.
