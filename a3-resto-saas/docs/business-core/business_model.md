# Business Model & Monetization Strategy

AK Business OS operates on a hybrid monetization model combining platform licensing, modular subscription additions, and marketplace revenue shares.

## 1. Platform Core Tier (SaaS License)

- **Description:** Encompasses multi-tenant onboarding, tenant isolation context, user profile dashboards, and core authentication structures.
- **Billing Metric:** Flat monthly subscription (per tenant seat/user count).

## 2. Modular Capability Upgrades

- **Description:** Tenants subscribe only to modules their organization requires (e.g. advanced inventory, POS terminal access, accounting tools, or payroll processing).
- **Billing Metric:** Capability-specific usage metrics (e.g. payment volume caps or active employee counts).

## 3. Developer Marketplace Commission

- **Description:** Third-party developer entities distribute custom industry packs and specific integrations via the Business OS Marketplace.
- **Billing Metric:** Rev-share split (e.g. 80/20 split) on recurring subscription sales processing through our billing pipelines.
