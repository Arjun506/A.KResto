# Business Vision

This document details the business and product strategy for **AK Business OS**.

## 1. Product Scope

The AK Business OS platform comprises three main branches:
- **Business Platform:** Multi-tenant dashboard offering modular SaaS applications (CRM, POS, Accounting, HRMS) to manage operations.
- **Consumer Platform:** Customer-facing services (e.g. checkouts, mobile reservation systems, QR portals) that integrate directly with business data.
- **Developer Platform:** Open APIs, webhook hooks, and SDK kits that allow developer communities to build custom capabilities.

## 2. Monetization Strategy

- **Core Tier:** Base access to business entities, authentication, and core settings.
- **Capability Subscriptions:** Pay-per-module subscription splits (e.g. CRM $10/mo, Advanced Analytics $25/mo).
- **Marketplace Shares:** Revenue share structure with third-party pack developers listing integrations.
